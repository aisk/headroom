# Privacy Policy

Headroom does not collect, store, or send any personal data.

- Headroom has no servers, accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or ads.
- To show your usage, Headroom runs the AI coding CLIs already installed on your computer (Antigravity, Claude Code, Codex, Grok) and reads what they print. The account email, plan, and usage figures they report are shown in the panel and kept only in memory while Headroom is running.
- Those CLIs talk to their vendors' services as they normally do; that traffic is covered by each vendor's own privacy policy. Headroom does not read your credentials and does not contact any service itself.
- The only files Headroom writes are a working folder for the Claude Code probe and `settings.json` (which tools are shown and how often usage refreshes), both under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Headroom`. The "Start with Windows" setting is stored by Windows.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/aisk/headroom/issues.
